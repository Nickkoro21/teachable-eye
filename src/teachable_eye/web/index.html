teachable-eye

Show it a few examples. It remembers — on your own device.

  1. Point the camera at something; boxes appear around what it sees.
  2. Choose a box (tap it; tap again for the next one under your finger).
  3. Teach it a name, or use Video to capture several views while you move around it.
  4. Check — green means known, orange means unsure, grey means unknown.

The PC is not answering.
On the phone, turn Tailscale on (it must say Connected). On the PC, the server starts by itself after sign-in; keep the PC awake. This page checks again every few seconds.

Everything it learns stays on your PC. Faces are stored only with the person's consent and can be forgotten at any time.
